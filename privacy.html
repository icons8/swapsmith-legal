Swapsmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are. Swapsmith ("the App") is an Android app provided by Generated Media, Inc. ("we", "us", "our"), 313 N Oak St, Roanoke, TX 76262, United States. You can contact us at startup@generated.photos. Analytics data from the App is kept in a Google Analytics account owned by Icons8, which also holds analytics for the icons8.com website.

In short. The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. To make a face swap, the App sends your photos to our servers and service providers. We also use analytics, crash reports and ads, as described below.

Photos and results. When you add a photo, the App sends it to our content check service, which uses OpenAI to check it for content that is not allowed. If the photo passes, the App uploads it to our storage, hosted by Cloudflare, and our face swap server uses it to make your result. Each result also goes through the content check before you see it. The App keeps your history of results on your device.

Reports. If you report a result, the App sends us the result image and details such as the reason you chose, your comment if you write one, and the App version. We use reports to find and stop misuse.

App ID. When you first open the App, it creates a random ID. We use it to sign the App's requests to our servers, to keep your uploads in a separate folder in our storage, and as your user ID in analytics.

Analytics. We use Google Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the App is used: the screens you open and the actions you take, the subscription plans you select and their price, the plans you buy or start a trial of, the App ID described above, a Firebase app instance ID, a Firebase Installations ID, information from Google Play about how the App was installed (install referrer), device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version, and approximate location (country, region and city) that Google derives from your IP address. Where the law requires consent, analytics follows your choice in the consent form described under Ads: until you answer, or if you decline, Firebase Analytics works in a limited mode and does not store analytics identifiers on your device. Our Google Analytics account has Google signals turned on: if you are signed in to your Google Account and have turned on ads personalization, Google may link this analytics data to your Google Account. The account also shares data with Google products and services and is linked to Google Ads with ads personalization turned on. Google uses this data as an independent controller to improve its own products and services, including advertising and ads personalization, and to detect invalid ad traffic. Google processes this data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Crash reports. We use Sentry, on a server we run at sentry.icons8.com, to collect crash reports and performance data so that we can find and fix errors.

Support logs. If you turn on logging in the App's hidden support screen and export the log, the App uploads the log file to our storage on Amazon Web Services. The log records what the App does, such as errors, ad and content check events, and can include your App ID and links to the photos you added. We use it to investigate problems with the App.

Ads. The App shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google collects your IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data and uses them for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law requires it, the App asks for your consent with Google's consent form before it loads ads. The same choice also applies to analytics. You can change your choice in Privacy Options in the Profile menu, next to About.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

Who receives your data. OpenAI (content check), Cloudflare (photo storage and network), Amazon Web Services (support logs) and the hosting providers of our servers process data for us. Icons8 owns the Google Analytics account where analytics data from the App is kept and has access to this data. Google provides Firebase Analytics and also uses analytics data for its own products and advertising as an independent controller, as described under Analytics. Google also receives data for ads (AdMob) and payments (Google Play) under its own privacy policy. Our content check service, face swap server and Sentry are run by us on rented servers.

Data retention. We keep the photos you upload to process your requests and to review reports and misuse, with no fixed storage period. Google Analytics keeps analytics data for 14 months; when you use the App again, this period starts over.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have rights over your personal data under local data protection law, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you have questions about your privacy, contact us at startup@generated.photos.

Children. Swapsmith is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us at startup@generated.photos.

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
